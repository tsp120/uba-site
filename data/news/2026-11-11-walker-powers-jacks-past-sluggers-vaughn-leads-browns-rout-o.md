September 26, 2026

Louisville (UBAN) - Christian Walker went yard and had 3 RBI at Sluggers Park as the Jacksonville Jacks beat the Louisville Sluggers by the count of 4 to 3.

Jacksonville took the lead for good in the 3rd inning scoring 2 runners on 4 hits. Louisville tried unsuccessfully to come back in the 9th.

Nolan McLean(10-2) picked up the victory, allowing 1 run in 5 innings. Wandy Peralta got the save, his 1st. The losing pitcher was Ranger Suarez(8-14). He allowed a few too many baserunners giving up 9 hits and 2 walks in 4 and 2/3 innings.

Chicago (UBAN) - Andrew Vaughn launched 2 homeruns and had 3 RBI at Otter Park as the St. Louis Browns beat the Chicago Otters by the count of 13 to 6.

Vaughn had a big day against Chicago. He clubbed a two-run home run in the 2nd inning, blasted a solo shot in the 6th inning and ripped a one-base hit in the 9th inning. Offensively, St. Louis was a juggernaut. They scored in 6 separate innings.


The victory was credited to Jason Alexander(6-1) who went 6 innings, allowing 3 runs. Dustin May(8-9) was hit with the loss. He was unable to control the St. Louis offense and allowed 8 hits and 3 walks in 4 and 1/3 innings.

New York (UBAN) - Tyler Wells went 6 solid innings as the Toledo Mud Hens defeated the New York Highlanders by the score of 3 to 1 at Babe Ruth Park.

Wells(3-4) was very good in the win. He allowed just 1 hit and no walks in 6 innings. Wells chalked up 9 strikeouts dropping his ERA from 3.71 to 3.38. Toledo had only 3 hits for the night.

Wells got help from Kenley Jansen who earned his 30th save. The loss was charged to Ryan Yarbrough(4-2). He pitched 6 innings and surrendered 3 hits and 3 walks.
