title: "Welcome to the new UBA website"
date: 2026-11-11
---
The league site has been rebuilt from the ground up. Everything still comes straight from the commissioner's Strat-O-Matic files. The league runs on his PC exactly as it always has; the website just shows what he publishes.

What's new:

- **Game stories every day**, pulled automatically from the Strat newspaper, back to 2014
- **Rosters as real tables** you can sort, plus a download of the original spreadsheet
- **Trade Assistant** that lets you tick players on up to three rosters and totals the salaries
- **Real MLB stats** next to every player's UBA line on the team stats pages
- Works properly on phones
