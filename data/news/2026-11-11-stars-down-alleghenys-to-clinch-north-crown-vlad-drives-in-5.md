September 27, 2026

Milwaukee (UBAN) - Steven Cruz performed beautifully in relief and Eugenio Suarez slammed a homerun and had 2 RBI at Miller Park as the Milwaukee Stars beat the Pittsburgh Alleghenys by the count of 4 to 1.

Milwaukee jumped on top of Pittsburgh in the bottom of the 3rd inning plating one baserunner on 2 base hits. That early scoring was all that Janson Junk(8-3) needed. Milwaukee managed only 5 hits on the afternoon.

Junk was helped out by Cruz who recorded his 1st save. Brad Keller(3-3) suffered the loss in relief. He allowed 2 hits and 1 walk in 2 and 2/3 innings.

Salt Lake City (UBAN) - First baseman Vladimir Guerrero Jr slammed 2 homeruns and had 5 RBI at Saints Field as the Washington Federals pulled off a dramatic comeback beating the Salt Lake City Saints by the count of 7 to 4.

Washington scored the winning runs in the top of the 9th inning when they tallied 4 runs on 5 hits. The key at-bat was by Guerrero Jr who deflated the crowd as he lost the ball over the fence for three runs. Salt Lake City was out-hit by Washington, 12 hits to 7.

Eric Orze(5-0) got credit for the victory, pitching 1 inning and allowing 1 run. Trevor Megill recorded the save, his 37th. Orion Kerkering(1-6) was charged with the loss in relief.

Guerrero Jr was jubilant after the game 'Let me see a box-score' he joked, 'I want to see how that game looks on paper.'

Indianapolis (UBAN) - Center fielder Pete Crow-Armstrong ripped 2 homeruns and had 3 RBI and Connelly Early whiffed 13 batters as the Indianapolis Longhorns defeated the Kansas City Monarchs by a score of 4 to 3 at Longhorn Stadium.

Crow-Armstrong enjoyed a fine evening at the plate. He ripped a base-hit in the 1st inning, hit a bases-empty home run in the 3rd inning and smashed a two-run homer in the 4th inning. Kansas City threatened in the 9th but Indianapolis was able to put down the rally.

Early(3-1) got the win allowing 1 run in 7 innings. Cooper Criswell was credited with his 7th save. Kevin Gausman(19-8) was the loser. He served up 3 gopher balls in 6 innings.

When asked about his strikeout total for the game, Early offered, 'The fan's really got into it. It is fun to see them tally up the strikeout totals up there in the stands. Fortunately, I kept them busy tonight - of course, it would be all for naught without the 'W'.'
